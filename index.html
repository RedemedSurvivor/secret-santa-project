    What changed after your feedback

    • No names on the wheel. Every wedge is a question mark, so nothing shows before the card.
    • The wheel is heavy and huge. It is wider than the phone stage and centred. It winds back, builds up slowly, rumbles, and takes about nine seconds to give up.
    • You cannot skip the spin. The skip link is gone.
    • Long drumroll, then fireworks. The roll starts while the wheel is still crawling, builds for about four seconds, then fireworks go off and the card pops up through them.
    • The big cursive only lives on the opening page. It writes itself when the link opens, then hands over to Hiii Cousins at the top. The small logo you liked stays in the corner on every page after that.
    • The letters are whole now. The first build was cutting the dots off the i’s and j as it wrote. Fixed.
    • Portraits redrawn from scratch. Shaded skin, real eyes with a proper wink, lips, lashes, hands with fingers, and clothes. Try the builder below.
    • Font. Geist Serif is not on the font service this page is allowed to load, and it is not in the Geist package either. I used Geist for text and Instrument Serif for headings. Send me the Geist Serif file and I will swap it in.

    Five answers I need before launch

    1. Who is in? The sheet’s title says 19 cousins in 7 branches. The rows list 24 in 8. I tested the draw with all 24 and it works.
    2. Code shape. The handoff says two words and two digits but shows “Mango 47”. I built two words plus two digits, like “Mango Lantern 47”. One word would be guessable in minutes.

    Other calls I made

    • Sound starts on. It begins with the first tap and one tap mutes it. All sound is made in the browser, so there are no audio files and no licences to clear.
    • Coming back shows the card, not the wheel. A second visit with the same code goes straight to the same cousin, with “Watch the spin again” for the show.
    • Sealed envelopes on the server (proposed, not built yet). Each match is locked with that cousin’s own code. Hosts get a list of who has opened and a nudge message to paste into WhatsApp. There is no screen, for anyone, that lists matches.

    Pitfalls pass by the Haiku reviewer

    A separate, cheaper model read the whole build and the screenshots cold and listed 12 problems. The fixes are in. One of its points, the wheel giving the match away early, is now moot because the wheel has no names.

    • FixedClosing the card left the name and portrait sitting in the page. It is now wiped on close and when the tab is put away.
    • FixedThe host nudge said “open the link” and had no link. It now carries the address, and shows the text to copy by hand if copying is blocked.
    • FixedA slow drag always spun clockwise. It now follows your finger.
    • FixedMusic kept playing with the phone locked or the tab hidden. It now pauses.
    • FixedSmall buttons raised to a full finger size. The theme button now shows a sun or a moon. A cousin who lost their code is told who to message. The gift date is one setting, not typed into the card.
    • Your callSound on from the first tap. The reviewer wants it off by default because of drumrolls in quiet rooms. I still think on is right for this crowd.
    • Your callShared phones. A returning code skips the wheel and shows the card straight away. Fine if codes stay private. Say so if siblings share a phone and I will always ask for a spin.
    • Before launchThe host view is open to anyone in this demo. The live one needs a host-only code checked by the server.

    Not tested by anyone yet: a real iPhone or Android keyboard over the code field, very large system text, and how smooth the wheel is on an older phone.

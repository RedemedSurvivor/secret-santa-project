# Njithi Cousins reveal · shooting script

| Time | Picture | Sound |
| --- | --- | --- |
| On page open | Write Njithi Cousins across a velvet title card, then draw two ink strokes underneath. Cut to the personalized invite or returning greeting. | Silence until a tap. |
| 0–0.9 s after spin | The prize wheel starts with weight under a fixed gold pointer. The face tilts as the rim catches light. | Quiet sax and spaced mechanical ticks. |
| 0.9–2.5 s | Match the turning rim to a car tire, with a passing road line and graphite spokes. | Sax stays low. |
| 2.5–3.4 s | Pull back to the Ferrari-inspired silhouette. It drives out through illustrated tire smoke. | The ticks thin out. |
| 3.4–5.2 s | Bring the road wheel back through the smoke. Match it to the painted wheel and ease it to rest beneath the pointer. | Last isolated tick. |
| 5.2–6.7 s | Velvet closes, the scene swaps in the dark, then the cloth parts. | Switch to the celebration excerpt at the opening. |
| Reveal | Open on the portrait and role, with fireworks above the copy and the reminder beneath the card. | Chorus at celebration level. |

Prioritize portrait framing. Keep the rim continuous through every match cut and preserve the original illustration direction across palettes. The mobile interaction is the primary version. The HyperFrames sequence provides a separate seekable wheel study.

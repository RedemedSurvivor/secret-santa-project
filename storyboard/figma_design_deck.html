Slide 01 · Design Knowledge Base Invariant

The 2+1 Color Hierarchy & Mood Architectures

Derived from Niki Aroca and Dharu Designs (Design Knowledge Base reference 04): Interfaces fail when juggling too many competing hues. We enforce the 60% Canvas / 30% Structural Surface / 10% Disruptive Accent rule, with an intentional 120° triadic color wheel turn to deliver electric personality without visual clutter.

1. Holiday Velvet & Gold 👑
60% Plum Deep · 30% Mulberry · 10% Tangerine / Gold
60% Canvas Base #1C0B1F
30% Structural Surface #3B1742
10% Disruptive Accent #FF6A3D (Tangerine)
Secondary Detail Accent #FFD166 (Gold Trim)

Mood: Deep holiday royalty, warm celebration, festive family prestige.

2. Triadic 120° Accent Shift 🌿
120° Wheel Turn from #FF6A3D -> Cyber Mint #10B981
60% Pine Forest Base #0D1F18
30% Structural Emerald #1C3D31
10% Triadic Accent (+120°) #10B981 (Mint)
Luminous Highlight #34D399 (Cyber Pine)

Mood: Evergreen spruce, energetic modern tech clarity, fresh festive balance.

3. Minimalist Linen & Cobalt 🏛
60% Warm Paper · 30% Slate · 10% Electric Cobalt
60% Warm Linen Paper #FBF9F5
30% Obsidian Slate #111827
10% Offguard Accent #0038FF (Cobalt)
Human Saffron Warmth #FFB703 (Saffron)

Mood: High-end fashion editorial, museum-grade layout, striking clarity.

Slide 02 · Modern Agency Typography

Typography Hierarchy & Pairing Rationale

Banning generic default Arial/Helvetica fonts in favor of geometric clarity and editorial serif prestige:

PRIMARY INTERFACE & BIO

Figtree & Geist Sans

Wide geometric counters, ultra-clear legibility on mobile viewports, warm friendly posture for cousin quips and button labels.

EDITORIAL HEADINGS

Instrument Serif

Refined modern serif that evokes classic print cards, high-end stationery, and dramatic festive invitations.

SIGNATURE BRANDING

Njithi Cousins

Hand-penned cursive wordmark at the header; acts as an instant clickable link back to home.

Slide 03 · Motion & Theatrical Staging

The Theatrical Red Velvet Curtain Reveal

A genuine theatrical velvet curtain transition. As the wheel decelerates, the crimson curtains draw together with heavy fabric physics and gold trim, pause in total suspense, then sweep wide as fireworks burst and Tyla’s *"Water"* chorus kicks in.

✦ Act II · Red Velvet Theatrical Draw ✦
01 Silent Wheel Spin: Clean mechanical rotation without frantic noise or fast drumrolls.
02 Crimson Curtains Draw Closed: CSS-accelerated velvet drapes (`#b8001f` to `#3a0008`) cover the stage with vertical pleats and gold piping.
03 Theatrical Parting & Boom: Curtains snap open wide, colorful multi-burst fireworks explode.
04 Tyla Chorus Takes Center Stage: Muffled, soothing audio (`reveal.mp3`) starts playing only as the card pops into view.
